# How to read this map

Use the atlas for a first look and comparison, not an emergency decision.

## Overlap is not damage

The figures describe intersections between mapped records and historical flood-model footprints. They are not observed flooding, predicted closures, population exposure or expected losses. The scenarios are alternatives, not a timeline.

The [current results](../README.md#current-results) use a broader health cohort than the earlier hospital-tag-only version. It includes clinics, centres, medical practices and rehabilitation records. Most records are not independently checked, and a source tag does not establish an official clinical classification. Four parking/animal-care candidates are held out for review. [Inventory review](inventory-review.md).

## More map detail is not a finer flood model

The KSDMA flood grid is still approximately 90 m. It cannot establish house-level safety, flood depth, bridge safety or road passability. The 30 m Copernicus layer is a surface model that can include buildings and vegetation—not bare-earth terrain. Relief shading adds context only; it did not refine the flood footprint or infer drains.

The mapped waterways are incomplete. A missing line does not mean no drain exists, and a mapped channel does not establish its depth, capacity or current flow. Smaller roads are not fully included. Coastal flooding, urban drainage and vulnerability need separate assessment. Blank map areas are not proven safe.

## Boundaries and area centres are approximate

The source snapshot contains 70 gram panchayats, seven municipalities and one corporation. Count agreement with the district list does not validate every name or boundary. These community polygons are not presented as a current statutory survey.

The location dots are inside-area locators, not office coordinates, shelters, entrances or safe points. Small gaps between district and local-body polygons are retained in an explicit `UNMATCHED` table row. Facility assignment is attribution by representative location, not a new flood-hazard test. Tables describe mapped counts; they are not vulnerability rankings.

## No observed-event validation is claimed

No verified district-covering observed flood layer is included. This is a data gap, not evidence that Kozhikode was dry. The model has not been validated against an observed event by this project.

## Not a live warning service

The atlas does not ingest new rainfall, send alerts or establish current facility operation. It is not an evacuation or routing tool. Use official authorities and current local information for decisions that affect safety.
